How to try

  1. Type a sentence in the message, in Portuguese or English, or use a sample button.
  2. Stop typing: after a short pause AIR writes one version per tone.
  3. Tap Right Ctrl (press and release it alone): pick mode, the first version is selected.
  4. Move with ↓ ↑ (↑ on the first goes to the last) and press Enter; or press 1 2 3; or click a row. Esc closes. Any other key just types. Pick mode closes after 4 s without a key.
  5. Ctrl+Z after applying brings your text back.
  6. The arrow beside the gear is blue while the bubble follows the text cursor. Drag the bubble by its bar and it stays there, with the arrow grey; click the arrow to switch between the two. The gear, or a click on the tray icon, opens the settings: on and off, the tones (check, order with the arrows or by dragging), layout, theme and the AIR key. With AIR off, the AIR key turns it on.
