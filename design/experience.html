How to try

  1. Type a sentence in the message, in Portuguese or English, or use a sample button.
  2. Stop typing: after a short pause AIR writes one version per tone.
  3. Tap Right Ctrl (press and release it alone): pick mode, the first version is selected.
  4. Move with ↓ ↑ (↑ on the first goes to the last) and press Enter; or press 1 2 3; or click a row. Esc closes. Any other key just types. Pick mode closes after 4 s without a key.
  5. Ctrl+Z after applying brings your text back.
  6. Tap Right Shift (the off key) puts AIR to sleep: the bubble closes, nothing is read, and the model's memory is freed (with Ollama answering, this page asks it to unload the model, as AIR does). The AIR key wakes it and loads the model again.
  7. The arrow beside the gear is blue while the bubble follows the text cursor. Drag the bubble by its bar and it stays there, with the arrow grey; click the arrow to switch between the two.
  8. The gear, or a click on the tray icon, opens the settings, a menu with its pages: General (on and off, the AIR key and the off key, each a tap or a shortcut you record), Tones (check, order with the arrows or by dragging), Bubble (layout, theme, follow), Engine (switching frees the old model) and About.
